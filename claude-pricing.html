How it works

  1. Free 30-minute call We talk about the problem and decide if I am a good fit for it.
  2. Discovery, if the scope is unclear A small paid phase turns ideas into a written scope. 50% of its fee is credited to the project.
  3. Quotation and agreement You get a written quotation and Statement of Work: scope, exclusions, milestones, acceptance criteria and price.
  4. Work in milestones You pay 40% at signing, 40% when you accept the milestone and 20% at handover. Projects below 10,000,000 VND are paid 50% at signing and 50% at handover.
  5. Handover and warranty You get the source code, documentation and all access in your name. I fix defects in the accepted scope free for 30 days.
  6. Maintenance, if you want it A monthly plan with scheduled preventive work and a fixed number of hours.

Packages

Hourly work

For any work without a package: advice, troubleshooting, small changes or a second pair of eyes. One flat rate of applies to all of it. We agree an hour cap before the work starts. You pay for the actual hours, in 30-minute steps with a 1-hour minimum, and you get a time log.

Maintenance

A monthly plan reserves scheduled time for your systems. All work counts against the included hours. Extra hours cost and need your approval first. Unused hours do not roll over. The minimum term is 3 months, then the plan renews monthly with 30 days' notice to stop.

Support terms

I work on a part-time schedule. On weekdays, support work happens at 7:30–9:00, 16:00–18:00 and 19:00–21:00, Vietnam time. At weekends I do planned work by agreement, such as cutovers and onsite visits. There is no support on public holidays or during Tết. I am not a 24/7 provider and do not guarantee uptime or restoration times.
