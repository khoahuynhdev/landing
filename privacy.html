Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-07

khoahuynh.dev is a static website. It has no user accounts, no forms and no scripts. I do not collect personal data from visitors through it. Cloudflare, described below, processes some request data.

Cookies and tracking

This site itself sets no cookies. It uses no analytics, no advertising tools, no tracking pixels and no third-party fonts or content networks. Every page loads only from this domain. Cloudflare, which sits in front of the site, may set strictly necessary security cookies (see below).

Hosting and request data

The site is a static website hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare serves it and may process your IP address, the time of your request and your browser user agent. It may also set strictly necessary security cookies to protect the site. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy for how Cloudflare handles this data.

I do not run analytics. I have no access to visitor logs beyond what Cloudflare provides.

Email

If you write to me, I receive your email address and the content of your message. I use it only to reply to you and do not share it with others.

Questions

For any question about this policy, write to hello@khoahuynh.dev.
